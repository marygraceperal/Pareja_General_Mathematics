Discussion Sheet

Learner-friendly explanation and worked example

Learning Target: I can solve gross-to-net income problems using a spreadsheet.

Essential Question

How can this mathematical idea help me understand or solve a real situation?

Let's Connect

Think of a situation in your home, school, community, or future workplace where this idea could be useful.

Key Concepts

Annual salary ÷ 12 or 52; hourly pay; overtime; commission; piecework; gross and net income.

Important Formula / Rule

Monthly = annual salary ÷ 12; weekly = annual salary ÷ 52; overtime pay = hourly rate × overtime multiplier × overtime hours; commission = sales × rate; gross pay = total earnings; net pay = gross pay − deductions.

Worked Example

Problem: An employee has an annual salary of ₱360,000. Find the monthly salary.

Answer: ₱30,000

Check: Identify the units and explain what the result means.

Common Errors

  • Choosing a formula before identifying the quantities and conditions.
  • Skipping units or conditions in the final answer.
  • Rounding too early.
  • Giving a numerical answer without interpreting it when the problem is contextual.

Check Your Understanding

  1. What is the main idea?
  2. Which quantity is unknown?
  3. Which formula or rule applies?
  4. How can you check whether your answer is reasonable?

Key Takeaways

Understand the situation first, choose the appropriate mathematical model, calculate carefully, and interpret the result in context.
